## UC-FA-PR-001: Annual Review Cycle
**Actor**: HR Manager (FA User)

**FA-Specific Flow**:
1. Initiate review cycle in FA
2. Manager completes reviews via FA forms
3. Self-reviews in employee portal
4. HR approves in FA
5. Compensation adjustments via FA-HRM

---

## UC-FA-PR-002: Performance Report
**Actor**: Management

**FA-Specific Flow**:
1. Generate performance reports in FA
2. Filter by department (FA dimension)
3. View ratings, goals, 360 feedback
4. Export for board presentation
